How you get started

  1. Find your school. Type its name. Any school that uses Canvas works.
  2. Sign in the way you always do. Your school's normal login. Kairos never sees your password.
  3. Your classes show up. Due dates, grades, and syllabus pages, sorted into one plan.

You'll need a Mac and Google Chrome, because sign-in happens in Chrome. Kairos for Canvas, a small Chrome extension, also shows what changed right on your Canvas dashboard.

Where your stuff lives

On your Mac

Your study history, Canvas sign-in, and grade math stay in a folder on your computer. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on.

Sent only when you ask

Optional AI help sends only the material you choose and the question or answer you ask about. It never sends your sign-in and can't change your grade.

Kairos won't submit, comment, or post anything in Canvas for you. It has no tools for instructors, doesn't scrape your school's student portal or RateMyProfessors, and has no streaks to lose.
