# Priority rubric

Student-owned ranking defaults used by task brief and week plan. The student
overrides are filled in Settings. These are preferences, not Canvas facts.

## P1 — do first

- Due within 48 hours and worth meaningful points.
- Exam, presentation, project, or external-tool work with a hard deadline.
- Work that directly supports a stated career or academic priority.

## P2 — plan next

- Due within 7 days.
- Medium/high-point work without an immediate deadline.
- Prerequisite or checkpoint preparation.

## P3 — fit around P1/P2

- Low-point routine work.
- Optional practice, readings, and administrative tasks.

## Student overrides

- Worth-by-default courses: (add in Settings)
- Throwaway / low-attention courses: (add in Settings)
- Time constraints: (not set)
