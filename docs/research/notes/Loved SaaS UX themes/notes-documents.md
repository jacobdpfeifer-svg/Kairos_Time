# Notes, documents, and personal knowledge UX (October 2026)

Research pass dated 13 October 2026. Scope is commercial notes, documents, and personal-knowledge products. Email, task managers, and design tools are a boundary only. Ratings, prices, and user counts appear only when a fetched source states them. Where two sources disagree, both are listed. Revenue and user counts that were not found are marked unknown.

## Which products both sell and have a repeated reputation for an interface people like?

### Takeaway

The clearest overlap of a real paid product and repeated praise for the interface itself is a small set of writing surfaces: Craft, Bear, iA Writer, Ulysses, and Typora. Notion and Obsidian have the large paid or paid-add-on bases, but reviewers praise the model (databases, local files, links) more than a calm first-run interface. Apple Notes is repeatedly called the interface people already like, and it is free inside Apple’s platform rather than a paid notes SKU. Google Docs, OneNote, Coda, and Confluence have large commercial bases; reviewers describe them as familiar or powerful, not as loved writing interfaces. Evernote still sells, and the interface reputation in 2025–2026 sources is poor.

### Inferences

- “Interface people like” and “large paid base” are different filters. Craft, Bear, iA Writer, Ulysses, and Typora pass the interface filter on repeated independent descriptions of a specific interaction. Notion and Obsidian pass the commercial filter and get qualified interface praise.
- Apple Notes belongs in the loved-interface set and fails a strict “paid notes product” test. It is included because reviewers treat it as the default people actually enjoy, and because it ships inside a commercial platform.
- Slite is the nearest team-wiki miss for the deep list: G2 ease of use 9.3 versus Confluence 7.9, but only 262 G2 reviews, and the praise in the sources is “the editor stays out of the way,” not a documented interaction pattern with the specificity of iA’s focus mode or Heptabase’s card library.

### Gaps

- No revenue, ARR, or paying-subscriber counts were found for Notion, Obsidian, Craft, Bear, Ulysses, or Heptabase. The only user-scale sentence found is iA’s “over two million writers,” which is a vendor claim.
- No MacStories review and no Setapp listing were fetched in this pass.
- Notion’s two G2 numbers (4.7/5,470 vs 4.6/13,834) were not reconciled. The 5,470 figure is from the reviews page whose body was saved; the 13,834 figure is from a G2 AI product page snippet.
- Bear’s marketing page lists iCloud sync under Free and also labels iCloud sync as Pro. The App Store still sells Pro at $2.99 / $29.99. What the free tier includes in October 2026 was not settled from one consistent official list.
- The historical team-docs product Almanac (the peer of Coda and Slite) was not found as a live notes product. A different 2026 Y Combinator company at usealmanac.com is an agent-and-wiki alpha on a waitlist as of 7 September 2026, not a notes editor. — [thisandthat.chat](https://www.thisandthat.chat/blog/almanac-review/)

## For a shortlist of ten, what is the product for, what interaction is praised, and how is that interaction built?

### Takeaway

The ten below are the deep shortlist. Each entry separates the job, the interaction reviewers name, and the mechanics a primary page or store listing actually describes. Praise of “beauty” is kept only where a source names the control: tags, sentence dimming, live render, blocks, cards-on-a-whiteboard, or a local folder.

### Cited Findings

#### Craft — documents that are also a light workspace

- Job: personal and shared documents, tasks, and collections, with offline apps plus web. Windows and web clients are described by a 2026 review as having arrived in 2025. — [App Store, Craft](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/craft-notes-documents-ai/id1487937127); [Apps Tested](https://appstested.com/best-note-taking-apps-2026/)
- Praised interaction: block documents that look like designed pages, with animation and type treated as part of the product. Reviewers say the block model is gentler than Notion’s and that exported PDFs are sendable. — [Calmevo](https://calmevo.com/craft-app-review/); [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/focus/best-note-taking-apps-2026/)
- How it is built: page/block editor, not an outliner. A release note on the App Store listing says reactions and comments can be added from the slash menu. Official docs index Collections (properties), document styling, tasks, publishing, and daily notes exposed to API/MCP. A comparison describes whiteboards as infinite canvases inside Craft, and says Collections are newer and thinner than a Notion database. Storage is the vendor’s sync with offline editing, not a folder of Markdown the user owns. — [App Store, Craft](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/craft-notes-documents-ai/id1487937127); [Craft docs](https://support.craft.do/); [YouTube comparison transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tKEwpU7djXM)
- Still disliked in these sources: weaker databases than Notion, and (in older framing that 2025–2026 reviews say is easing) a history of being Apple-first. — [Calmevo](https://calmevo.com/craft-app-review/)

#### Bear — tagged Markdown notes on Apple devices

- Job: capture, notes, light wikis, and writing, for one person across Apple devices. — [bear.app](https://bear.app/); [App Store, Bear](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bear-markdown-notes/id1016366447)
- Praised interaction: open a note and type, file it with a tag, and stay in a theme. An App Store review describes welcome pages, less than a day to feel fluent, and an “elegant and cozy” UI, and contrasts that with bouncing off Obsidian’s intro video. — [App Store, Bear](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bear-markdown-notes/id1016366447)
- How it is built: a note is a Markdown document, not a block database and not an outline of bullets. Organization is nested tags, not a folder tree as the primary model. The info panel holds a table of contents and backlinks. Headings fold to hide sections. Themes are a first-class setting (the site says three themes on the free tier and Pro tag icons). Sync is iCloud; the vendor says it cannot read notes. Sketches sit inline. There is no collaboration surface; a 2026 review states that directly. — [bear.app](https://bear.app/); [App Store, Bear](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bear-markdown-notes/id1016366447); [Calmevo](https://calmevo.com/craft-app-review/)
- Empty state: the store listing says no signup and no account. The same review says the welcome pages were enough for the basics.

#### iA Writer — one sentence on a page

- Job: drafting prose in Markdown, then exporting. Not a knowledge base. — [ia.net/writer](https://ia.net/writer)
- Praised interaction: Focus Mode. The official description is highlight the sentence or paragraph you are in and fade everything else. Syntax highlighting colors parts of speech. A 2026 review says this is the most precise focus mechanic in the category. John Gruber’s quote on iA’s page praises Markdown syntax styling and iA’s own typefaces (Mono, Duo, Quattro). — [ia.net/writer](https://ia.net/writer); [BestWriting](https://bestwriting.com/distraction-free-writing-apps)
- How it is built: plain files, custom monospaced (and related) faces, almost no chrome. Writing and preview are separate modes. Style Check is on-device and marks clichés and fillers without rewriting. Authorship tracking paints typed words differently from pasted and AI text. Focus Mode, per an older description iA still publishes, can drop spellcheck and cut-and-paste while it is on. There is a seven-day trial. Storage is the file system (iCloud, local, or other folders), not a proprietary library. — [ia.net/writer](https://ia.net/writer)
- Empty state is the product. iA’s page says a blank page is both liberating and intimidating, and the UI refuses fonts, toolbars, and title chrome.

#### Ulysses — a library of sheets for Apple writers

- Job: many drafts, goals, proofing, and publishing to WordPress, Ghost, and Substack, on Mac, iPad, and iPhone only. — [App Store, Ulysses](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulysses-writing-app/id1225570693); [Apps.Deals](https://blog.apps.deals/ulysses-vs-scrivener-ia-writer-bear-mac)
- Praised interaction: a sidebar of sheets and groups that can get out of the way, plus a distraction-free editor, writing goals, and an editing assistant that flags repetition and style. — [App Store, Ulysses](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulysses-writing-app/id1225570693); [BestWriting](https://bestwriting.com/distraction-free-writing-apps)
- How it is built: not files in the user’s folder by default. The store listing says work lives in “Ulysses’ unified library,” with sync across Apple devices and feature parity. Reviewers call the markup Markdown XL, a proprietary flavor, which is the lock-in complaint next to the subscription. Export targets include PDF, Word, and ebooks. — [App Store, Ulysses](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulysses-writing-app/id1225570693); [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/focus/best-writing-apps-2026/)
- Onboarding: a trial that includes export and sync, per the store text. Reviewers say people delay starting because of the subscription. — [BestWriting](https://bestwriting.com/distraction-free-writing-apps)

#### Typora — Markdown that looks formatted while you type

- Job: editing Markdown files as a person who does not want a separate preview pane. Cross-platform (macOS, Windows, Linux in the 2026 reviews). — [typora.io](https://typora.io/); [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/focus/best-writing-apps-2026/)
- Praised interaction: live render. Syntax characters drop away as you type; headings, tables, math, and diagrams appear in place. One reviewer dislikes that same behavior because a heading becomes large immediately and pulls attention to presentation. — [typora.io](https://typora.io/); [Mayhem Code](https://www.mayhemcode.com/2026/08/ia-writer-vs-ulysses-vs-scrivener-in.html)
- How it is built: a file-based editor. Side panel is a file tree plus an outline that jumps to headings. Focus Mode blurs other lines; Typewriter Mode pins the caret line to the middle. `#tags` are not a native organizer; global search can find the characters. Themes are CSS. There is no vendor library and no built-in sync; users put the folder in Dropbox or iCloud themselves. — [typora.io](https://typora.io/); [Typora support, 6 Sep 2026](https://support.typora.io/File-Management/)

#### Obsidian — a folder of Markdown plus optional plugins

- Job: a personal knowledge base the user can leave with, on desktop and mobile. Teams are possible only through shared vaults in paid Sync. — [obsidian.md/pricing](https://obsidian.md/pricing); [App Store, Obsidian](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/obsidian-connected-notes/id1557175442)
- Praised interaction: `[[` links between notes, a graph, and the fact that the files are ordinary Markdown. Roundups praise durability and plugins. App Store reviews praise tabs on desktop and Dataview-style queries, and they say the app gets out of the way only after Markdown is familiar. — [Apps Tested](https://appstested.com/best-note-taking-apps-2026/); [App Store, Obsidian](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/obsidian-connected-notes/id1557175442)
- How it is built: local-first. A vault is a folder. Core pieces named in the iOS release notes include daily notes, Bases, themes, and a restricted mode that opens a vault with plugins off. Graph view and community plugins are on the store description. Sync is optional and end-to-end encrypted; iCloud vaults are the other path the iOS app names. The vendor says it does not collect telemetry and cannot read Sync contents. — [obsidian.md/pricing](https://obsidian.md/pricing); [App Store, Obsidian](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/obsidian-connected-notes/id1557175442)
- Empty state: a new vault has notes only if the user creates them. Reviewers call the curve front-loaded. There is no house template marketplace in the sources fetched here; structure comes from community plugins and the user’s own folders.

#### Notion — blocks, databases, and a workspace tree

- Job: docs, notes, tasks, and wikis for a person or a team, in one cloud workspace. G2’s product text says customization is the positioning. — [G2, Notion reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/notion/reviews)
- Praised interaction: a page that can become many block types, then a database with views. Reviewers say the surface feels friendly and that, once a workspace is configured, small teams rate it highly (G2 ease of use listed at 90% on the “worth it” page). — [learn.g2.com](https://learn.g2.com/is-notion-worth-it); [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/notion/reviews)
- How it is built, as reviewers describe it rather than as an official interaction spec (the Notion help center was not fetched): pages in a sidebar tree; databases are the organizing primitive and are where relations, rollups, and formulas live; the friendly typing surface and the database builder are different skills. A 14-day 2026 test says pages felt natural in two days and relations did not. Offline is described by a February 2026 secondary compilation as limited (that compilation says offline mode shipped August 2025, with a 50-row limit and no browser offline). Treat that offline detail as secondary. — [Accelrio](https://www.accelrio.com/notion-review-2026/); [Try or Bye, Notion](https://www.tryorbye.com/products/notion)
- Empty state: templates are the intended on-ramp, per G2’s product description (“start with a template, or build from scratch”). Reviewers say the blank workspace plus the number of block types is why people buy courses. — [G2, Notion reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/notion/reviews); [learn.g2.com](https://learn.g2.com/is-notion-worth-it)

#### Apple Notes — the note that is already open

- Job: quick capture on Apple devices, including scans, images, audio, checklists, and PDFs, synced with iCloud, including on the web. — [Mayhem Code](https://www.mayhemcode.com/2026/08/ia-writer-vs-ulysses-vs-scrivener-in.html)
- Praised interaction: zero setup. Open, type, done. Roundups call it the right tool for people who do not want a knowledge system. — [Apps Tested](https://appstested.com/best-note-taking-apps-2026/); [awe.cool](https://www.awe.cool/blog/every-note-app-compared-2026)
- How it is built: folders and tags, not backlinks. One 2026 guide says each note is an island and search is keyword (plus handwriting OCR). That guide’s “no AI” claim should be checked against Apple’s own 2026 notes; this pass did not fetch an Apple support page. Markdown import is claimed by the August 2026 comparison, not by Apple in a page fetched here. — [awe.cool](https://www.awe.cool/blog/every-note-app-compared-2026); [Mayhem Code](https://www.mayhemcode.com/2026/08/ia-writer-vs-ulysses-vs-scrivener-in.html)
- Commercial signal: bundled and free. No notes subscription was found.

#### Heptabase — cards that sit on whiteboards

- Job: learning and research for someone who arranges notes spatially. Paid, with a seven-day trial. — [Heptabase pricing](https://support.heptabase.com/en/articles/12990121-plans-and-pricing); [VibeReference](https://www.vibereference.com/product-and-design/notetaking-personal-knowledge-tools)
- Praised interaction, in the vendor’s own usage guide more than in independent reviews fetched here: put cards on a whiteboard, group them into sections, and keep the long text inside the card. — [Heptabase organize guide](https://wiki.heptabase.com/organize-knowledge-and-projects)
- How it is built: a card library is the store. Whiteboards do not own cards; placing a card on a board does not move it out of the library. Inside a card, `/` inserts headings, lists, to-dos, toggles, tables, media, code, math, and dates; `@` mentions. On the board, tools include select, note card, connect (arrows), text, section, journal, mind map, and sub-whiteboard. The vendor’s onboarding prescription is a two-layer board tree (Projects, Topics, Life, and so on, then sub-boards), tags only for archival records, and sections (`Cmd/Ctrl+G`) before a third layer of boards. They say to wait until a board has more than about 100 cards. That is an official answer to the blank-canvas problem. — [Fundamental elements](https://wiki.heptabase.com/fundamental-elements); [Organize guide](https://wiki.heptabase.com/organize-knowledge-and-projects)
- Independent UI essays and store ratings for Heptabase were not fetched. A secondary pricing page lists early-bird Pro at $9.99 and several yearly figures; the extract was too garbled to treat those yearly totals as clean facts.

#### Capacities — typed objects instead of a page tree

- Job: a personal knowledge base organized by kinds of things (pages, people, books, and user-defined types), with a free tier and paid Pro. — [CostBench](https://costbench.com/compare/capacities-vs-reflect/); [Pickuma, 2026](https://pickuma.com/for-pm/reflect-vs-tana-vs-capacities-engineers-2026/)
- Praised interaction in a 2026 engineer comparison: the object model matches bibliographies and CRM-like notes, and the free tier is usable for solo work. The same piece says you should not pick Capacities if you need plugins or local-first files. — [Pickuma](https://pickuma.com/for-pm/reflect-vs-tana-vs-capacities-engineers-2026/)
- How it is built: every note is an object with properties. Body text is a Content property made of blocks. `/` or `+` creates blocks and objects; `@` reuses an existing object. An object block can render as a title, a card, or an embed that is editable in place (transclusion). Blocks can sit in columns. Cloud, not a Markdown folder; export is described as Markdown plus JSON. AI is on the paid tier in that comparison. — [Capacities blocks](https://docs.capacities.io/reference/blocks); [Pickuma](https://pickuma.com/for-pm/reflect-vs-tana-vs-capacities-engineers-2026/)

### Inferences

- The interfaces reviewers call intuitive are the ones that pick a single gesture and refuse a second organizing system: tag a note (Bear), dim a sentence (iA Writer), render the mark you just typed (Typora), or drop a card on a board that does not become the file system (Heptabase).
- Notion and Obsidian are loved after configuration. Their empty states are a workspace or a vault, and the praise starts once links or databases exist.
- Local-first versus cloud splits the shortlist cleanly. Obsidian, Typora, and iA Writer are files. Bear is iCloud. Craft, Notion, Heptabase, Capacities, and Ulysses keep a vendor library. Apple Notes is iCloud.

### Gaps

- No official Notion help page was fetched, so slash-menu details, wiki layouts, and the 2026 offline spec are not from Notion itself.
- No independent Heptabase review with a rating was fetched. The spatial interaction is documented by Heptabase, not confirmed by a critic in this pass.
- Capacities and Reflect prices come from a comparison site that says it verified them in October 2026, not from a fetched capacities.io pricing page.
- iA’s App Store price ($19.99) and the August 2026 per-platform prices ($29.99–$49.99) were not reconciled. They may be different storefronts.

## What do reviewers still dislike?

### Takeaway

The repeated complaints are a blank or overwhelming start, slow databases, lock-in, a steep second layer under a friendly surface, subscriptions, and sync. They attach to different products. Minimal editors are criticized for having nowhere to put a long project. Powerful workspaces are criticized for the weeks before the workspace works.

### Cited Findings

- Blank page and blank system. iA treats the blank page as the point and also quotes the intimidation. Obsidian reviewers and roundups describe a front-loaded curve and a plugin blank slate. Notion reviewers say the UI is friendly and then “incredibly dependent” on databases that take days of tutorials. G2 attributes 23% of concern mentions to learning curve and setup. Heptabase’s own guide exists because an empty whiteboard gets a third layer of boards too fast. - Dislike clusters by what the product refused to leave out. Writing apps are disliked when the library is proprietary or billed forever.
