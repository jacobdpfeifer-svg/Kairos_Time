Private beta · CU Boulder classmates · invitation only

A local-first study companion. It reads your Canvas course material, builds short practice checks from it, tells you why each one was chosen, and keeps honest track of what you have actually retained. Your answers and history stay on your computer.

Download

No installer is published yet. The macOS build exists as an unsigned bundle and has not passed a fresh-machine test; Windows has not been built. This page will list a checksum-verified installer only after that testing is done. Nothing here is a placeholder link.

Supported when released: macOS 12+ (Apple silicon first). Chromebooks and phones are not supported by a desktop app.

What it does

  • Study: one source-backed question at a time, with a plain explanation of why it was picked. Deterministic checks where a key exists; honest abstention where it does not.
  • Sources: import your own Canvas pages, syllabus and assignment descriptions after signing in with your school account; write your own questions on top of them.
  • Plan: due-date overview and an optional Google Calendar study block you confirm before it is created.
  • AI help (optional, beta-funded): provisional feedback on an answer, never a grade that changes your schedule.

What it does not do

  • It never submits assignments, posts to discussions, or acts on Canvas for you.
  • It does not solve live or proctored assessments; material for those is refused at import.
  • It does not claim you are “exam-ready”. Counted checks are delayed, unaided answers only.

Privacy

Your study history is stored locally, per profile, on your device. When you ask for AI help, selected material and your answer pass through our service to the model provider (Anthropic Claude). Our service is designed not to retain that content and keeps only request ids, token counts and status; provider retention policies still apply and are disclosed by the provider. Optional usage counts (session started/finished) are off until you enable them. Canvas cookies, Google and Microsoft tokens stay in your profile folder and are never sent to the model.

These statements describe the software as built and tested locally. Service-side no-retention claims will be marked verified only after deployment checks with synthetic canary content.

Support

Beta testers: reply to your invitation message. Include the app version from Settings → Diagnostics and what you were doing. Please do not paste answers or course content into support messages.

Recovery: quitting the app keeps your open attempt and draft. To reset a profile, delete its folder (shown in Settings → Diagnostics) while the app is closed.
